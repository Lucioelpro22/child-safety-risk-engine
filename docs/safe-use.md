# Safe use

`child-safety-risk-engine` is an assistive, deterministic signal generator. It is not a finding that grooming, abuse, or a crime occurred, and its score is not a calibrated probability.

Consumers must keep a human reviewer in the loop for high and critical results, preserve an appeal path, and avoid automatic blocking, reporting, family contact, or law-enforcement contact based only on this score. Do not use the engine for clinical, legal, employment, education-admission, or emergency decisions.

The engine is designed to minimize data. Do not log submitted messages, identifiers, exact birth dates, or full conversation histories. Configure retention and deletion in the consuming system, and restrict access to assessments involving minors.

Absence of a detected signal does not prove that a conversation is safe. Rules should be evaluated for language, culture, context, and false positives before deployment.
